# Viva Questions and Answers

| # | Question | Simple English | Roman Urdu |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is inventory? | Goods a business keeps for sale or operations. | Inventory woh maal hai jo business sale ya operation ke liye rakhta hai. |
| 2 | What is an anomaly? | An observation unusual compared with learned behavior. | Anomaly woh record hai jo learned normal pattern se different ho. |
| 3 | Is this classification? | No; there is no trusted target label. | Nahi, kyun ke trusted target label mojood nahi. |
| 4 | Is this regression? | No; the main output is unusualness, not a numeric target prediction. | Nahi, output prediction nahi balkay unusualness score hai. |
| 5 | Why unsupervised learning? | The dataset has no reliable anomaly labels. | Dataset mein reliable anomaly labels nahi hain. |
| 6 | What is KDE? | A density estimate; low density suggests rarity. | KDE density estimate karta hai; low density rare behavior hota hai. |
| 7 | What is GMM? | A mixture of Gaussian patterns that gives likelihood. | GMM multiple Gaussian patterns se likelihood nikalta hai. |
| 8 | What is PCA? | It compresses many features into fewer components. | PCA bohat features ko kam components mein convert karta hai. |
| 9 | What is K-Means? | A clustering algorithm; distance can support comparison. | K-Means clusters banata hai; center se distance comparison mein madad deta hai. |
| 10 | Why use multiple algorithms? | Different views of unusualness are combined. | Mukhtalif methods ki views combine hoti hain. |
| 11 | What is an anomaly score? | A standardized 0–1 priority measure. | 0 se 1 tak unusualness ki priority measure. |
| 12 | How is threshold selected? | Default is the 95th percentile of training scores. | Default training scores ka 95th percentile hai. |
| 13 | What is feature engineering? | Creating useful signals from raw columns. | Raw columns se useful signals banana. |
| 14 | What is data leakage? | Using future or target information improperly. | Future ya target information ghalat tareeqay se use karna. |
| 15 | Why scaling? | Distance and density models need comparable feature magnitudes. | Distance aur density models ko comparable scale chahiye. |
| 16 | Why RobustScaler? | It is less affected by extreme values. | Extreme values ka asar comparatively kam hota hai. |
| 17 | Why Flask? | It provides a lightweight Python web application. | Flask lightweight Python web app deta hai. |
| 18 | What happens on upload? | Validate, clean, engineer, transform, score, explain, display. | File validate, clean, features, transform, score aur display hoti hai. |
| 19 | Does upload retrain? | No; it uses saved artifacts. | Nahi, saved models use hote hain. |
| 20 | What does high stock-to-sales mean? | Possible overstock or slow movement. | Overstock ya slow movement ka signal ho sakta hai. |
| 21 | What does low coverage mean? | Possible replenishment or stockout risk. | Replenishment ya stockout risk ho sakta hai. |
| 22 | Why not remove outliers first? | They may be exactly the records we need to detect. | Woh records hi detection ka target ho sakte hain. |
| 23 | Is anomaly equal to fraud? | No, it needs human investigation. | Nahi, human investigation zaroori hai. |
| 24 | What is the data source? | Original Kaggle dataset, CC0, synthetic-but-realistic. | Original Kaggle dataset hai, CC0 aur synthetic-but-realistic. |
| 25 | What is a SKU? | A product identifier. | Product ka unique identifier. |
| 26 | What is inference? | Applying saved models to new data. | Saved model ko new data par apply karna. |
| 27 | What is a KPI? | A measurable business performance indicator. | Business performance ka measurable indicator. |
| 28 | What is silhouette score? | A clustering separation/cohesion measure. | Clusters ki separation aur cohesion ka measure. |
| 29 | What are limitations? | Synthetic data, false positives, threshold sensitivity. | Synthetic data, false positives aur threshold sensitivity. |
| 30 | How can it improve? | Real data, alerts, monitoring, database, access control. | Real data, alerts, monitoring, database aur access control add kiye ja sakte hain. |
